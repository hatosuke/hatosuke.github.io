Lincoln Bus

Privacy Policy

StarTran arrivals, schedules, live buses, and nearby stops

Lincoln Bus helps riders in Lincoln, Nebraska check StarTran bus stops, timetables, live vehicle locations, and arrival estimates. The app is designed for quick everyday checks before commuting, going to school, or getting around town.

Main Features

Nearby Stops

Find StarTran stops near your current location and open stop details with a tap.

Timetables

View stop timetables by route. Past departures are dimmed so the next buses are easy to spot.

Live Bus Locations

Check where buses are currently running and see arrival estimates for supported StarTran routes.

Favorite Routes

Save frequently used stops and routes so you can check the next bus faster.

Widgets and Premium

Premium is a one-time purchase that removes ads, unlocks widgets, and allows unlimited favorite routes.

Data

This app uses StarTran / City of Lincoln, NE transit data and GTFS Realtime feeds provided through Connexionz.
